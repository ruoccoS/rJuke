# Changelog

All notable changes to rJuke are listed here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-10

The first public release of rJuke, for Android (phone, tablet, Android TV, Android Auto) and Windows.

### Highlights
- **Music app**: home with album of the day and daily picks, fast library with artists, albums and tracks, instant search, animated Now Playing, light and dark theme.
- **Synced lyrics** from tags or `.lrc` files, line by line and word by word.
- **Playlists**: create and reorder, import `.m3u8` / `.m3u` with smart matching and a review of the tracks that weren't found, automatic import from the music folder, export to `.m3u8`.
- **PartyBox**: guests scan a QR code and pick tracks from their browser, with open queue or approval, per-guest limits and guest management.
- **rJuke Connect**: devices on the home Wi‑Fi find each other, control each other and move playback; custom name and icon for each device.
- **PC as a speaker**: use the phone's library on a PC, with a track cache and the phone serving music even when locked.
- **Android TV** interface for the remote, with music from the TV, a USB drive or another device.
- **Android Auto** with browsing, voice search, steering-wheel seeking and PartyBox controls.
- **English and Italian** interface.
