rJuke {{VERSION}}
================

GETTING STARTED
1. Extract the whole "rJuke" folder wherever you like (for example in Documents).
   Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click rJuke.exe.
3. On the first launch Windows may show "Windows protected your PC":
   click "More info" and then "Run anyway". It only happens the first time,
   because the executable isn't digitally signed.
4. Tip: right-click rJuke.exe > "Pin to Start" or "Pin to taskbar".

FIRST SETUP
You'll be asked for your name and where you keep your music:
- "In a folder on this PC": pick the folder (subfolders are scanned too);
- "On my phone": see PC AS A SPEAKER below.
Name, music source, language and theme can be changed at any time in Settings.

PC AS A SPEAKER (MUSIC FROM THE PHONE)
Handy on a PC where you don't want to copy your whole library (at the office, say).
With the PC and the phone on the same Wi-Fi network and rJuke open on the phone,
pick the phone from the list and tap "Allow" on the phone. The PC receives the track list,
with covers, lyrics and playlists: search and browsing work as usual.
Tracks are fetched from the phone when you play them; the ones you played (and the next two
in the queue) stay on the PC up to the limit chosen in Settings (1, 2, 5 or 10 GB),
so they start right away even if the phone doesn't answer.
Note: some office or "guest" networks don't let devices see each other;
in that case use the phone's hotspot.

FORMATS
MP3, FLAC, M4A (AAC), OGG, OPUS, WAV.
Note: M4A files in ALAC format (Apple Lossless) and WMA files can't be played.

SYNCED LYRICS
Read from the file's tags (LRC in USLT/LYRICS/UNSYNCEDLYRICS, SYLT frame) or from an .lrc file
with the same name as the track. Word-by-word lyrics (enhanced LRC) are supported too.

PLAYLISTS
Import .m3u8/.m3u files from the sidebar (arrow icon next to "Playlists") or from Settings.
Add tracks with the "..." menu or by dragging them onto the playlist. Reorder by dragging.
.m3u8/.m3u playlists found in the subfolders of the music folder
are imported automatically at every scan (without duplicates).

RJUKE CONNECT (PC <-> PHONE)
With the PC and the phone on the same home Wi-Fi network and rJuke open on both,
the "Devices" button (at the top, next to Lyrics) shows the phone: you can control it,
bring the music to the PC ("Listen here") or send it to the phone ("Move the music").
On the first launch Windows will ask whether to allow rJuke network access:
tick "Private networks" and click "Allow access", otherwise the phone won't see the PC.
The first time a device connects, rJuke asks for permission.

PARTYBOX
The confetti button at the top starts "<your name>'s PartyBox": a QR code appears.
Your friends scan it with their camera and, from their phone's browser (without installing anything),
pick tracks to add to the queue. You can choose between an open queue and approval.
They need to be on your same Wi-Fi network.
If the phones can't open the page, the PartyBox panel shows
"Allow rJuke": press it and answer "Yes" to the Windows prompt.

SHORTCUTS
Space play/pause · Ctrl+Right/Left arrow next/previous track
Right/Left arrow forward/back 5 s · Up/Down arrow volume
Ctrl+F search · Ctrl+L lyrics · Esc close
The keyboard's media keys work too.

DATA
Settings, library and playlists are saved in %APPDATA%\rJuke
(your music files are never modified).
